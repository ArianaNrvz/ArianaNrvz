Cluster Lashes

Everything you need to know before your first set.

What are cluster lashes?
Small sections of 3 to 8 lash strands joined at the base, applied one at a time for a custom look.
How long do they last?
Up to 7 days with lash bond, and several sets of wear if you clean and store them properly.
How do I apply them?
Place the cluster close to your natural lash line using a lash bond, then press it gently to your lashes for a few seconds.
How do I remove them?
Soak a cotton pad in oil-based remover, hold it on your lashes for 20 seconds, then slide the clusters off gently.
Cluster lash
noun
[kluhs-ter lash]

Lash bond
noun
[lash bond]
a lash-safe adhesive that holds clusters to your natural lashes
